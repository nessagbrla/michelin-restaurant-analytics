03 / Selected Findings

Three patterns that stood out.

32.3%
Japan's starred share

359 of 1,113 Michelin-listed restaurants in Japan are starred, giving the market one of the strongest star concentrations among major countries in the dataset.

65.1%
Creative cuisine starred

691 of 1,061 restaurants categorized as Creative cuisine hold at least one Michelin star.

2.94%
Green Star recognition

577 of the 19,622 restaurants analyzed hold Michelin Green Star recognition in the dataset.

05 / Behind the Dashboard

How I built the analysis.

Data Engineering

Built Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers in Databricks to separate raw data from standardized and analytics-ready datasets.

Transformation

Standardized international price symbols, derived Michelin star indicators, structured geographic fields, and created a deterministic SHA-256 restaurant identifier.

Data Quality

Reconciled row counts and validated unique IDs, required fields, geographic coordinates, price tiers, and Michelin star logic before promoting data to the Gold layer.

Business Intelligence

Connected the Gold dataset from Databricks to Power BI and built DAX measures, synchronized filters, geographic visualizations, and comparative market analysis.
